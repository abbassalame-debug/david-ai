# Demo Bistro overview

Synthetic note for the demo vault. Demo Bistro is a fictional shawarma and wraps shop used by the restaurant example.

- Opening hours: 11:00 to 01:00, seven days a week.
- Sells chicken and beef shawarma wraps, platters by weight, fries, drinks and a few combo offers.
- Chicken and beef are cooked on spits; the closing weigh-in records what is left.
- Cash is counted every night in US dollars and in local-currency notes; a difference is reported, never hidden.
- The weekly question for the manager: is the meat that was cooked equal to the meat that was sold plus the waste?
