# Studio checklist (demo)

Synthetic note for the demo vault; it shows how a second vault is kept apart from the business notes.

1. Set the sample rate to 48 kHz and the buffer to 128 samples before recording.
2. Arm one track at a time; check the input level peaks around -12 dBFS.
3. Record two full takes, then comp the best phrases.
4. Bounce stems at the project sample rate; keep the dither off until the final master.
5. Park any idea that comes up mid-session in the incubation note instead of chasing it.
